## Round 3 — taste

**Fable:** Picture the person who lands on the home page cold: they have a job to do and will give it five seconds. Who is that person, by job, and what is the one sentence they say out loud after those five seconds?

**AI Builder:** Priya, accounts-payable lead at a 300-person manufacturer, forty invoices a day, a controller who asks her why a payment was wrong. Her company's policy says no documents leave the building, so every "AI invoice" tool she has seen is already off the table before she reads the second line. After five seconds she says: *"It reads our invoices here, on our own machine, and it shows me where each number came from."* If she says "it's an AI extraction platform", we failed.

**Fable:** Name the bar. Not a category — a page I can open.

**AI Builder:** Four, and I mean the pages, not the brands. **linear.app**, the home fold: one sentence, the product itself in the frame, nothing else fighting for the eye — that is the restraint I want above the fold. **stripe.com**, the whole page: that is what a company that is actually selling looks like — navigation with real destinations, proof, security, integrations, a footer you could run a business from. Complete. **mercury.com**: finance software that is warm and confident instead of corporate or "AI"; the copy talks like a person. And the sample I gave you, the editorial with the plates — for the middle of the page, where each section opens with a drawing and a "how you know it worked". For the review screen: **Linear's issue view** — density that reads as calm — and **Figma's inspect panel**, because evidence should sit on the canvas next to the thing it is evidence of.

**Fable:** What would make you close the tab?

**AI Builder:** A hero illustration of nothing — abstract blobs, a robot, a glowing brain. The word "AI-powered". Three feature cards with icons. A dashboard screenshot shrunk to forty percent so I cannot read it. A percentage without its denominator. A dark theme that is just black with white text. Any word I would need your engineers to explain. A "book a demo" button before I have seen the product. And a page that could be for any product if you swapped the logo.

**Fable:** How complete does the page have to be?

**AI Builder:** As complete as a Series-C company's, because that is who I am comparing you to when I decide whether to trust you with our payables. Navigation I can actually use; the product in the hero; proof — and if you do not have customers, say so and show me the measured numbers and the open build instead, honestly labelled; a tour; where the numbers land — my books; security stated as facts I can send to our IT lead; a comparison with what I do today; pricing; the questions I would ask, answered; a footer with legal, status, and a way to reach a person. If any of that is missing I assume you are two people and a demo.

**Fable:** Three directions will be rendered for you to choose from. What must differ between them, so the choice is real?

**AI Builder:** The bet, not the colour. One should bet on the *product as proof* — the hero is the real review screen, large, and the page is a tour of it. One should bet on the *promise and the person* — Priya's day, the invoice on her desk, warmth, the product appearing only when the story needs it. One should bet on the *number* — the guarantee and the honest zero front and centre, the page as an argument to a controller. If I can tell them apart with the words covered, they are different enough.

**Fable:** Enough, or one more?

**AI Builder:** One more. Phone first. Half our approvals happen on a phone in a corridor. If the fold does not work at 390 pixels, I do not care about the desktop.

## Round 4 — verifiers

**Fable:** How will you know it worked, without reading code?

**AI Builder:** I will open it on my phone and my laptop and say the sentence. I will hand it to someone who has never heard of it — a real stranger, not your critic — and time them. I will look for the denominator under every percentage. I will approve one invoice end to end and check it stuck after a reload. And I will look at the queue after ten invoices and see whether I know what to do first without thinking.

**Fable:** What would make you reject a green run?

**AI Builder:** The fold not telling me what it sells. Any number I cannot trace to a row. A control I cannot reach on the phone. A screen I have to be taught. And this: if the page passes your critics and I cannot make sense of it, your critics are wrong, not me.

**Fable:** What is yours to choose, and what is mine?

**AI Builder:** Mine: which of the three, the headline sentence, what "done" means, and whether I trust it. Yours: everything else — decide it, write it down, do not ask me. But show me the choice before you build, every time.

**Fable:** Anything to work from?

**AI Builder:** What you already have: `apps/web/DESIGN.md`, `apps/web/design/positioning.md` — check its promise against Priya's sentence — the sample PDF, the transcript of the video, and the four pages I named. Fetch them and show me; do not describe them to me.

**Fable:** Enough?

**AI Builder:** Enough. Show me references.
